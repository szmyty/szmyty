🌐 ONTOLOGY

The canonical concept model of Ego Hygiene.

---

Purpose

The ontology defines the conceptual world of Ego Hygiene.

It does not describe implementation.

It does not describe Flutter.

It does not describe databases.

It defines the things that exist within the system and the relationships between them.

Every schema, specification, AI agent, implementation, and visualization should ultimately derive from this ontology.

---

Why an Ontology Exists

Software normally begins with implementation.

Ego Hygiene begins with understanding.

Before software can be built, we must first define:

- what exists
- how concepts relate
- how meaning emerges
- what the system is attempting to model

The ontology becomes the shared language between:

- humans
- AI agents
- specifications
- schemas
- implementations

---

Core Philosophy

Reality exists independently of software.

The ontology attempts to model reality.

Software is merely one implementation of that model.

As understanding evolves, the ontology may evolve.

Implementations should follow the ontology rather than redefine it.

---

Core Concepts

Domain

A Domain represents a major area of human life.

Examples:

- Mental & Emotional Health
- Physical Health
- Financial Health
- Relational Health
- Purpose & Identity

Domains organize human experience.

They do not implement it.

---

Practice

A Practice is a repeatable behavior that supports one or more Domains.

Examples:

- Reflection
- Gratitude
- Mindfulness
- Journaling
- Abundance
- Sleep Hygiene

Practices are how growth becomes embodied.

---

Ritual

A Ritual is an intentional composition of Practices organized around a specific context, purpose, or time.

Examples:

- Morning Ritual
- Evening Ritual
- Weekly Review
- Work Startup Ritual

Rituals are not the same as Practices.

Practices are reusable behaviors.

Rituals are intentional sequences of Practices assembled to serve a particular moment or intention.

Rituals span multiple Domains rather than belonging to a single Domain.

They provide structure, rhythm, and meaning across different areas of life.

---

Reflection

A Reflection captures conscious awareness of an experience.

Reflection transforms experience into understanding.

Reflection often produces Insights.

---

Insight

An Insight represents understanding extracted from one or more Reflections.

Insights connect observations into patterns.

Insights should influence future behavior.

---

Research

Research captures observations, hypotheses, experiments, and evidence.

Research exists before specification.

Research may promote into specifications when sufficient evidence exists.

---

Knowledge

Knowledge represents integrated understanding.

Knowledge emerges from:

Experience

↓

Reflection

↓

Insight

↓

Integration

Knowledge is not information.

Knowledge is information that has been understood.

---

Goal

A Goal represents a desired future state.

Goals provide direction.

Practices provide movement.

Reflection provides adaptation.

---

Journey

A Journey represents the evolving history of a person's development over time.

The Journey encompasses:

- practices
- reflections
- insights
- goals
- domains
- relationships
- emerging understanding

The Journey is not a point in time.

The Journey is the continuous record of growth, recovery, and learning across a person's life.

Journey is a first-class concept within Ego Hygiene.

The application should model a person's Journey rather than only their current state.

---

Progress

Progress exists at multiple levels within Ego Hygiene.

Practice Progress

The completion or engagement with individual practices over time.

Journey Progress

The accumulation of experiences, reflections, and insights that form a person's ongoing record of growth.

Understanding Progress

Deepened comprehension of oneself, one's patterns, and one's relationship with growth.

Understanding is as meaningful a form of progress as task completion.

Progress should be evaluated across all three levels rather than reduced to a single metric or streak.

---

Metric

A Metric represents an observable signal.

Metrics support awareness.

Metrics should never replace judgment.

Metrics exist to inform rather than control.

---

Module

A Module is a software implementation supporting one or more concepts.

Examples:

- Reflection
- Therapy
- Progress
- Financial Stewardship

Modules implement ontology concepts.

Modules are not ontology concepts themselves.

---

AI Assistant

An AI Assistant is a supporting participant within the system.

Its purpose is to:

- educate
- organize
- summarize
- reflect
- assist

AI should amplify human understanding rather than replace it.

---

Relationships

Domains contain Practices.

Practices support Domains.

Practices compose into Rituals.

Rituals span multiple Domains.

Practices produce Reflections.

Reflections produce Insights.

Insights reference Research.

Research informs Practices.

Practices influence Goals.

Goals influence behavior.

Behavior generates new Reflections.

Reflections and Insights accumulate into the Journey.

The Journey preserves the continuous record of growth over time.

The ontology forms a continuous learning system.

---

The Continuous Learning Loop

Experience

↓

Reflection

↓

Insight

↓

Understanding

↓

Practice

↓

Behavior

↓

New Experience

This loop represents the primary growth mechanism within Ego Hygiene.

---

Relationship to Other Artifacts

The ontology defines concepts.

Schemas define structure.

Specifications define behavior.

Agents perform implementation.

Implementations realize concepts.

Documentation explains concepts.

The ontology remains the highest-level model describing the system itself.

---

Design Principles

The ontology should remain:

- implementation-independent
- technology-independent
- human-readable
- AI-readable
- conceptually stable
- philosophically grounded

Changes to the ontology should be made deliberately.

Many implementation changes should not require ontology changes.

---

Evolution

The ontology is expected to evolve.

New concepts should be added only when they represent genuinely new ideas rather than implementation details.

The ontology should remain small, stable, and expressive.

---

Final Thought

The ontology is not a description of the application.

The application is one possible realization of the ontology.

If the implementation changes, the ontology should largely remain intact.

If the ontology changes, every downstream artifact should be evaluated for alignment.

The ontology is therefore the deepest source of truth within Ego Hygiene.
