⚖️ Principles

The enduring principles that guide every decision within Ego Hygiene.

---

Why Principles Exist

Purpose explains why Ego Hygiene exists.

Vision describes where it is going.

Pillars describe what areas of life it seeks to support.

Principles define how decisions are made.

Every architectural decision, feature, research direction, and implementation should be evaluated against these principles.

They are intended to remain stable even as technology evolves.

---

1. Awareness Before Optimization

Understanding comes before improvement.

The goal is not to optimize people.

The goal is to help people become more aware of themselves, their patterns, and their lives.

Optimization without understanding often creates fragility.

Awareness creates sustainable growth.

---

2. Alignment Over Productivity

Productivity is not the objective.

Alignment is.

The system should help users align:

- values
- intentions
- behaviors
- outcomes

A productive life without alignment is not the goal.

---

3. Stewardship Over Perfection

Growth is maintained through stewardship.

Not perfection.

Small, sustainable actions consistently applied are more valuable than unsustainable bursts of effort.

The system should encourage returning, not restarting.

Recovery is a natural and expected part of the growth process.

Missing a practice is an opportunity for renewed awareness, not evidence of failure.

The application should treat interruptions with compassion rather than punishment.

---

4. Research Before Architecture

Ideas should mature before becoming systems.

Observe.

Collect evidence.

Identify patterns.

Then create architecture.

Technology should emerge from understanding rather than replace it.

---

5. Simplicity Before Complexity

Complexity should only be introduced when it provides meaningful value.

Simple systems are easier to understand, maintain, and evolve.

Every new feature should justify its existence.

---

6. Local First

Personal knowledge belongs to the individual.

Whenever practical:

- data should remain local
- users should control their information
- synchronization should be intentional

Privacy is a design principle, not an optional feature.

---

7. Human-Centered AI

Artificial intelligence should support human judgment.

Not replace it.

AI should:

- summarize
- organize
- reflect
- educate
- assist

Final decisions should remain with the individual.

---

8. Build Foundations Before Features

Strong foundations reduce long-term complexity.

Architecture should support growth.

Features should emerge from architecture rather than define it.

Invest in systems that make future work easier.

When a capability can reasonably benefit future Flutter applications, design it first as reusable framework infrastructure.

Application-specific behavior should be implemented on top of that framework, not embedded into it.

Over time, the architecture should trend toward an intentional 80/20 split:

- ~80% reusable Flutter foundation
- ~20% Ego Hygiene-specific concepts

---

9. Evidence Over Assumption

Claims should be supported whenever practical.

Practices should be informed by:

- scientific research
- lived experience
- observation
- continual refinement

When evidence is uncertain, uncertainty should be acknowledged.

---

10. Integration Over Fragmentation

Human life is interconnected.

Mental health affects physical health.

Financial health affects relationships.

Sleep affects cognition.

The application should help users understand these relationships rather than isolate them.

---

11. Sustainable Development

The project itself should embody its philosophy.

Development should remain:

- maintainable
- documented
- reproducible
- collaborative

Avoid sacrificing long-term health for short-term velocity.

---

12. Continuous Learning

Neither the project nor its creators are finished.

Research continues.

Understanding evolves.

Architecture improves.

The system should remain open to refinement without losing its philosophical foundation.

---

13. Consistency Over Streaks

Long-term engagement is more valuable than unbroken performance.

The application should reinforce sustainable practice rather than perfectionist streaks.

Consistency is not the absence of gaps.

Consistency is the commitment to return.

Concepts such as continuity, recovery, and sustainable practice are more meaningful than a count of consecutive days.

Short recovery windows should be treated as natural rather than penalized.

The application should celebrate returning as much as beginning.

---

14. Compassionate Design

The application should reduce shame rather than amplify it.

Tone should remain supportive, calm, and nonjudgmental.

When a person returns after an absence, the response should welcome them back.

Not remind them of what they failed to do.

Examples:

Instead of:

"You lost your streak."

Prefer:

"Welcome back."

or

"Continue your journey."

The emotional environment of the application is as important as its features.

People should feel met where they are.

---

15. Multidimensional Progress

Progress is not one-dimensional.

Ego Hygiene recognizes progress at multiple levels:

- Practice Progress: engagement with individual practices
- Journey Progress: the evolving record of reflections, insights, and growth over time
- Understanding Progress: deepened self-knowledge and awareness

Completing a task is one form of progress.

Gaining understanding is another.

Returning after a difficult period is another.

All are valid.

All deserve recognition.

---

Applying These Principles

When making a decision, ask:

- Does this increase awareness?
- Does this improve alignment?
- Does this reduce unnecessary complexity?
- Does this strengthen the foundation?
- Does this respect user autonomy?
- Is this supported by evidence?
- Will this remain valuable over time?
- Does this support recovery rather than punish interruption?
- Does this reduce shame rather than amplify it?
- Does this serve the person's long-term journey rather than short-term metrics?

If the answer is consistently "yes," the decision is likely aligned with the philosophy of Ego Hygiene.

---

Final Thought

Technology changes rapidly.

Human needs evolve slowly.

These principles exist to ensure that Ego Hygiene continues serving people rather than chasing trends.

Every implementation should reinforce the philosophy.

The philosophy should never become subordinate to the implementation.
