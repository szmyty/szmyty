# Epistemology

> *How Ego Hygiene approaches knowledge, understanding, and uncertainty.*

---

# Overview

Ego Hygiene exists to help people cultivate a deeper understanding of themselves.

It does not attempt to provide ultimate answers about human experience.

Instead, it provides a framework for navigating uncertainty through reflection, evidence, personal experience, and multiple perspectives.

The goal is not certainty.

The goal is better understanding.

---

# Core Principles

## Curiosity Before Certainty

Curiosity creates space for learning.

Certainty can prematurely close it.

The application encourages exploration before conclusion.

---

## Evidence Before Confidence

When scientific evidence exists, it should be represented accurately and transparently.

When evidence is incomplete, evolving, or conflicting, the application should acknowledge uncertainty rather than presenting speculation as fact.

Confidence should reflect the quality of available evidence.

---

## Multiple Perspectives

Human experience has been explored through many lenses throughout history.

These include:

- scientific research
- philosophy
- psychology
- contemplative traditions
- cultural practices
- lived experience

No single perspective fully explains the complexity of being human.

The application may present multiple perspectives while clearly distinguishing between them.

---

## Observation Before Interpretation

The application should distinguish between:

- observations
- patterns
- interpretations
- hypotheses
- personal meaning

Observations should not automatically become conclusions.

Interpretations should remain open to revision.

---

## Personal Meaning

No system can fully define another person's subjective experience.

The role of Ego Hygiene is not to tell users who they are.

Its role is to help them better understand themselves.

Meaning ultimately belongs to the individual.

---

## Knowledge Evolves

Scientific understanding evolves.

Personal understanding evolves.

The application should remain adaptable as both continue to develop.

Learning is treated as an ongoing process rather than a finished destination.

---

# The Role of AI

Artificial intelligence does not replace human judgment.

Its purpose is to assist users by:

- organizing information
- identifying patterns
- providing context
- encouraging reflection
- presenting relevant perspectives

AI should never imply certainty beyond what is justified.

---

# The Role of the User

The user remains the primary expert on their own lived experience.

The application provides tools for reflection, exploration, and understanding.

It does not replace personal agency.

---

# Guiding Question

Rather than asking:

> "What is objectively true about this person?"

Ego Hygiene asks:

> "What helps this person develop a deeper, healthier, and more compassionate understanding of themselves while remaining open to learning?"

---

# Summary

Ego Hygiene is grounded in evidence where available, informed by diverse human perspectives, and centered on the individual's lived experience.

The application embraces curiosity, intellectual humility, and continuous learning.

Its purpose is not to eliminate ambiguity, but to help people navigate it with greater clarity, compassion, and wisdom.
