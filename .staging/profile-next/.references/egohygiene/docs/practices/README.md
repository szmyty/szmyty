🧘 Practices

The repeatable actions, reflections, and behaviors that support long-term well-being.

---

Overview

Practices are the active layer of Ego Hygiene.

If domains represent areas of life, practices represent things people actually do.

Examples:

- Gratitude
- Abundance
- Mindfulness
- Reflection
- Journaling
- Breathwork
- Budget Review

Practices transform philosophy into action.

---

Why Practices Exist

Knowledge alone rarely creates change.

Understanding must eventually become behavior.

Practices provide a bridge between:

Awareness
↓
Action
↓
Integration

Without practices, philosophy remains theoretical.

Without reflection, practices become mechanical.

Both are necessary.

Practice Design Principles

A practice should:

- be repeatable
- be understandable
- support awareness
- support integration
- require minimal cognitive overhead
- remain useful over long periods of time

A practice should not:

- depend on perfection
- require complex setup
- create shame when missed
- become a source of unnecessary pressure

Guiding Principle

Domains answer:

What area of life am I maintaining?

Practices answer:

What can I do today?

The purpose of a practice is not perfection.

The purpose is returning.

Consistently.

Gently.

With awareness.
