🔬 Research

Observations, hypotheses, experiments, and future directions.

---

Overview

Research exists to capture ideas before they become architecture.

Not every observation should become:

- a feature
- a module
- a specification
- an implementation

Research provides a safe place for exploration.

The goal is understanding.

Not immediate execution.

---

Why Research Exists

Many important ideas begin as:

- observations
- questions
- patterns
- frustrations
- intuitions

The value of research is preserving these ideas long enough to determine whether they are meaningful.

Research allows Ego Hygiene to remain curious without becoming chaotic.

---

The Lifecycle of an Idea

Most ideas should follow this progression:

Observation
↓
Research
↓
Repeated Evidence
↓
Hypothesis
↓
Specification
↓
Implementation
↓
Validation

Most ideas should remain in research longer than initially feels comfortable.

---

What Belongs Here

Research may include:

- cognitive observations
- design hypotheses
- architectural experiments
- domain investigations
- philosophical questions
- future possibilities
- notes from books
- notes from papers
- emerging patterns

Examples:

- Cognitive Navigation
- Context Preservation
- Attention Allocation
- Human-AI Collaboration
- Personal Knowledge Systems
- Personal Health Knowledge Engine

Current research artifacts:

- [Personal Health Knowledge Engine](./personal-health-knowledge-engine.md)

---

What Does Not Belong Here

Research should not contain:

- implementation commitments
- production requirements
- accepted architecture
- finalized specifications

Once an idea becomes stable, it should move elsewhere.

---

Research Categories

Observations

Things noticed repeatedly.

Example:

People struggle more with context than information.

---

Hypotheses

Ideas that seem true but require validation.

Example:

Many modern problems are coherence problems rather than information problems.

---

Experiments

Small attempts to gather evidence.

Examples:

- prototypes
- workflows
- journaling exercises
- AI-assisted systems

---

Future Directions

Potential long-term exploration areas.

Examples:

- Cognitive Navigation
- Personal Cognition Systems
- Context Graphs
- Intent Preservation

---

Research Principles

Observe Before Building

Not every insight requires immediate implementation.

Allow ideas to mature.

---

Evidence Over Excitement

Interesting ideas are not automatically good ideas.

Look for recurring evidence.

---

Preserve Context

Record:

- why an idea appeared
- what problem it addresses
- what observations support it

Future understanding depends on preserved context.

---

Avoid Premature Architecture

Architecture should emerge from validated understanding.

Research should not be forced into implementation too early.

---

Relationship to Specifications

A specification represents:

Decision

Research represents:

Possibility

Research should inform specifications.

Specifications should not replace research.

---

Guiding Principle

The purpose of research is not to create more ideas.

The purpose of research is to improve understanding.

Observe.

Collect evidence.

Identify patterns.

Allow good ideas to survive long enough to prove themselves.
