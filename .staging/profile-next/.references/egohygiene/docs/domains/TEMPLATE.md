Domain Template

Canonical structure for defining an Ego Hygiene domain.

---

Domain Name

«Replace this section with the domain name.»

Examples:

- Financial Health
- Mental Health
- Physical Health
- Relational Health

---

Overview

Describe the purpose of the domain.

Answer:

- What area of life does this domain represent?
- Why does it matter?
- How does it contribute to overall well-being?

---

Purpose

Describe the long-term role of the domain within Ego Hygiene.

Examples:

- Maintain physical capability and resilience.
- Improve financial stability and stewardship.
- Strengthen relationships and communication.

---

Core Principles

List the foundational ideas of the domain.

Example:

- Awareness before optimization.
- Sustainability over intensity.
- Progress through consistency.

---

Relationship to Other Domains

Describe major connections.

Example:

Financial Health
↓
Mental Health
↓
Relational Health

No domain exists in isolation.

---

Practices

List practices associated with the domain.

Examples:

Abundance
Budget Review
Cash Flow Reflection

or

Mindfulness
Journaling
Therapy

Each practice may eventually receive its own practice document.

---

Common Challenges

Describe challenges commonly encountered within the domain.

Examples:

- Burnout
- Scarcity thinking
- Emotional spending
- Social isolation
- Sleep deprivation

---

Desired Outcomes

Describe what healthy maintenance of the domain looks like.

Examples:

- Greater stability
- Improved awareness
- Better decision-making
- Increased resilience

---

Potential Metrics

Metrics should support awareness.

Metrics should not become the purpose.

Examples:

- consistency
- frequency
- trend
- balance
- satisfaction

Avoid reducing the domain to a single score.

---

Future Application Modules

Potential modules that may eventually support this domain.

Examples:

Wealth
Reflection
Movement
Relationships

These are implementation concepts, not the domain itself.

---

Future Features

Examples:

- tracking
- journaling
- insights
- visualizations
- recommendations

Features should emerge from the domain rather than define it.

---

Related Research

List useful references, papers, books, frameworks, or concepts.

Examples:

- Positive Psychology
- Cognitive Behavioral Therapy
- Behavioral Economics
- Systems Thinking

---

Open Questions

List unresolved questions.

Examples:

- What practices belong here?
- What metrics are useful?
- What modules should support this domain?

Avoid inventing certainty.

---

Notes

Additional observations, hypotheses, or future directions.

This section may contain research ideas that have not yet become specifications.

---

Summary

A domain defines:

- an area of life
- a maintenance responsibility
- a collection of practices
- a source of future insights

Domains are intended to remain relatively stable even as features, modules, and technologies evolve.
