🏛️ Domains

The major areas of life maintained through Ego Hygiene.

---

Overview

Domains represent the major systems that make up a human life.

They provide structure for:

- reflection
- practices
- insights
- tracking
- education
- future application modules

Domains are intentionally broad.

They describe areas of life, not specific activities.

---

Why Domains Exist

Most people do not experience life as a collection of disconnected tasks.

Life is experienced through interconnected systems:

- health
- relationships
- finances
- purpose
- creativity
- environment

Domains provide a stable structure for organizing these systems.

They help answer:

«Which area of life am I working on?»

---

Domain Design Principles

A domain should:

- represent a meaningful area of life
- remain stable over time
- support multiple practices
- support future application modules
- be understandable without technical knowledge

A domain should not:

- represent a single feature
- represent a temporary project
- represent a specific implementation detail

Future Application Relationship

Domains are expected to become major application modules.

Example:

Financial Health
↓
Wealth Module
↓
Abundance Practice
↓
Insights
↓
Visualizations

Another example:

Mental Health
↓
Reflection Module
↓
Journaling Practice
↓
Insights
↓
Patterns

The application layer should emerge from the domain layer.

Not the other way around.

---

Guiding Principle

Domains represent areas of life.

Practices represent actions.

Insights represent learning.

The purpose of Ego Hygiene is to help people maintain alignment across all three.
