# 🗺️ Ego Hygiene Roadmap

*An evolving map of current understanding, priorities, and long-term direction.*

---

## Purpose

This roadmap describes the current direction of Ego Hygiene.

It is not a contract.

It is not a promise.

It is not a feature list.

It is a living document that captures the current understanding of where the project is heading.

As understanding evolves, the roadmap should evolve with it.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

Build understanding before complexity.

Prefer:

Research

↓

Evidence

↓

Specification

↓

Implementation

instead of:

Idea

↓

Implementation

Technology should emerge from understanding rather than replace it.

---

## Current Priority

The current priority is not feature development.

The priority is building a coherent foundation that future features can grow from.

The architecture should be stable before the application becomes large.

### Rituals

Document intentional compositions of practices organized around specific contexts, purposes, or times.

Rituals are distinct from individual practices.

Practices are reusable behaviors.

Rituals are intentional sequences of practices assembled to serve a particular moment or intention.

Each ritual should eventually include:

* Purpose and context
* Constituent practices
* Domains spanned
* Recommended cadence
* Reflection integration

Examples include:

* Morning Ritual
* Evening Ritual
* Weekly Review
* Work Startup Ritual

Rituals span multiple domains and provide structure, rhythm, and meaning across daily life.

Status:

Beginning

## Version 1

The objective of Version 1 is not to implement every idea.

The objective is to produce a coherent, usable foundation that demonstrates the philosophy of Ego Hygiene.

Version 1 should include:

* Reflection
* Domains
* Practices
* Rituals
* Local-first storage
* Insight foundations
* Journey foundations
* Stable architecture
* Documentation
* Specifications

The application should already provide meaningful value before advanced AI capabilities are introduced.

---

## Foundation Extraction Milestone

After Version 1, the roadmap includes explicit framework extraction:

```text
Version 1.0
↓
Framework Extraction
↓
Reusable Flutter Foundation
↓
Future Applications
```

This extracted foundation should become the starting point for future projects rather than being recreated manually.

The architecture target remains:

- ~80% reusable Flutter infrastructure
- ~20% Ego Hygiene-specific capabilities

## Future Platform Evolution

After the foundation is complete, future work may include:

### Journey Layer

The Journey Layer models a person's evolving history over time.

Rather than only tracking current state, the application should preserve and surface the ongoing record of:

* practices
* reflections
* insights
* goals
* relationships
* personal growth milestones

The Journey should enable users to:

* understand how they have changed over time
* recognize patterns across their life
* revisit earlier reflections and insights
* appreciate their long-term growth

The Journey is a first-class concept rather than a background log.

---

### Insight Layer

* Pattern detection
* Reflection history
* Cross-domain insights
* Personal trends

---

### Knowledge Layer

* Knowledge ingestion
* Learning objects
* Research integration
* Artifact relationships
* Personal knowledge graph

---

### AI Layer

AI should support reflection rather than replace it.

Potential capabilities include:

* Reflection assistance
* Insight summarization
* Practice coaching
* Domain-specific guidance
* Knowledge synthesis

All AI integrations should remain provider-agnostic.

---

### Learning Layer

The Learning Layer connects educational resources to the rest of the system.

Learning resources may eventually connect:

* research
* practices
* domains
* rituals
* reflections

These are educational experiences rather than application features.

Learning resources should deepen understanding of practices and domains rather than prescribe behavior.

No implementation is required at this stage.

---

### Health Layer

Potential future integrations include:

* Health metrics
* Wearables
* Nutrition
* Sleep
* Exercise
* Laboratory data
* External health applications

The objective is not to replace specialized health tools.

The objective is to connect health information to reflection and long-term understanding.

---

### Cognitive Layer

Long-term research includes:

* Context preservation
* Attention allocation
* Cognitive navigation
* Intent preservation
* Human-AI synchronization

These concepts remain research until sufficient evidence supports implementation.

## Success Criteria

The roadmap succeeds if Ego Hygiene helps people:

* reduce cognitive load
* understand themselves more deeply
* maintain healthier practices
* connect knowledge with action
* build sustainable long-term growth

The application is only one expression of that goal.

The philosophy, practices, research, and community are equally important parts of the system.

## Guiding Thought

Build a framework worth understanding before building software worth scaling.

The technology should amplify the philosophy—not define it.
