🔭 Ego Hygiene Vision

A vision for a human-centered operating system for self-maintenance, reflection, and growth.

---

Vision Statement

Ego Hygiene aims to become a personal operating system for human flourishing.

Not a productivity app.

Not a therapy replacement.

Not a social network.

Not another collection of disconnected tools.

A coherent system that helps people understand themselves, maintain alignment, and navigate an increasingly complex world.

---

The Problem

Modern life produces more information than humans can meaningfully integrate.

People struggle with:

- information overload
- fragmented attention
- emotional dysregulation
- disconnected tools
- loss of purpose
- loss of coherence

The problem is rarely a lack of information.

The problem is often a lack of integration.

---

The Opportunity

Technology has become exceptionally good at:

- storing information
- generating information
- distributing information

Technology remains poor at helping humans:

- maintain context
- maintain alignment
- maintain self-awareness
- connect actions to values
- integrate experiences into growth

Ego Hygiene exists to help bridge that gap.

---

Long-Term Goal

Create a system that helps people:

- understand themselves
- understand their patterns
- maintain healthy practices
- preserve context across time
- make values-aligned decisions
- develop sustainable growth

The system should help people become more aware, not more dependent.

---

Core Principles

Humanity First

Technology should amplify humanity.

It should not replace it.

The goal is increased agency, not increased automation.

---

Awareness Before Optimization

Understanding comes before improvement.

Reflection comes before intervention.

The system should prioritize clarity before efficiency.

---

Local-First Thinking

Personal information should remain under the user's control whenever possible.

Privacy is a feature.

Ownership matters.

---

Alignment Over Productivity

The objective is not maximizing output.

The objective is maintaining alignment between:

- values
- intentions
- actions
- outcomes

---

Sustainable Growth

Growth should be:

- gradual
- intentional
- maintainable

The goal is not perfection.

The goal is stewardship.

Success Criteria

A successful Ego Hygiene system helps users:

- feel more grounded
- feel more aware
- make better decisions
- maintain healthy practices

- grow intentionally

Success is not measured solely through productivity.

Success is measured through sustainable alignment.
