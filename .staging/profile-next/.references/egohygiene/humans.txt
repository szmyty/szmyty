/* PROJECT */

Name: Ego Hygiene

Description:
A privacy-first, offline-first platform for cultivating self-awareness,
capturing knowledge, and transforming personal experience into
understanding through thoughtful software, research, and publishing.

Mission:
Help people better understand themselves through intentional reflection,
evidence-informed practices, and open source technology.

Core Principles:
- Privacy First
- Offline First
- Human Centered
- AI-Assisted, Not AI-Driven
- Evidence Informed
- Open by Default
