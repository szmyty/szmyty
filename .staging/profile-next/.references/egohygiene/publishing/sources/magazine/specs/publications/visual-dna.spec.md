Purpose

This specification defines the foundational visual identity of Ego Hygiene publications.

It serves as the highest-level aesthetic specification from which:

environments
layouts
components
templates
pages

inherit their visual characteristics.

This specification does NOT define:

specific page layouts
specific components
implementation details

Instead it defines:

«what makes an Ego Hygiene publication feel like Ego Hygiene.»

---

Core Philosophy

The publication should feel like:

a discovered artifact of self-understanding.

Not:

a corporate wellness brochure

Not:

a startup dashboard

Not:

a scientific journal

Not:

a generic self-help magazine

The publication should evoke:

mystery
discovery
transformation
embodiment
self-exploration
personal sovereignty
ancient wisdom
future possibility

The emotional feeling should be:

«"I found something meaningful."»

---

Visual Identity

The publication exists at the intersection of:

pulp magazine
mystical guidebook
cosmic self-development
visual mythology
reflective education

The publication should feel:

timeless
collectible
physical
handcrafted
intentional

The publication should never feel:

sterile
corporate
overly digital
generic
mass-produced

---

Material Language

The publication should feel physically printed.

Characteristics:

aged paper
distressed surfaces
subtle wear
ink imperfections
print artifacts
edge damage
grain
texture

Avoid:

perfectly clean surfaces
flat digital gradients
sterile vector aesthetics
clinical presentation

The artifact should feel:

«handled rather than rendered.»

---

Typography Language

Typography should communicate:

confidence
clarity
guidance
discovery

Headline characteristics:

bold
condensed
high-impact
vintage-inspired
poster-like

Body typography characteristics:

readable
grounded
approachable
educational

Avoid:

ultra-minimal luxury typography
corporate UI typography
thin fragile headings

---

Color Language

Primary emotional palette:

amber
gold
aged yellow
burnt orange

Primary environmental palette:

midnight blue
deep navy
cosmic teal
indigo

Accent palette:

magenta
violet
cyan
crystal-inspired highlights

Color should feel:

warm
luminous
mystical
alive

Avoid:

harsh neon overload
flat grayscale systems
sterile white interfaces

---

Lighting Language

Lighting is symbolic.

Light represents:

awareness
insight
growth
discovery
embodiment

Common lighting characteristics:

glow
bloom
halos
rim lighting
atmospheric illumination
radiant centers

Avoid:

flat illumination
purely functional lighting

---

Symbolic Language

Recurring symbols may include:

crystals
pathways
infinity symbols
human silhouettes
light sources
stars
cosmic environments
natural elements
transformation imagery

Symbols should support:

reflection
discovery
healing
growth

Symbols should not exist purely as decoration.

---

Illustration Language

Illustrations should feel:

mythic
symbolic
emotionally resonant
slightly surreal

Illustrations should communicate:

concepts
states
transformations

rather than literal realism.

---

Atmosphere

The publication should prioritize atmosphere.

Atmosphere is a first-class design system.

Atmosphere may communicate:

mystery
safety
empowerment
curiosity
reflection
transformation

The emotional environment is often as important as the content itself.

---

Visual Density

The publication should be:

rich
layered
detailed

but still:

readable
structured
navigable

Avoid:

minimalist emptiness
overwhelming chaos

Target:

«dense meaning with clear hierarchy.»

---

Emotional Tone

The viewer should feel:

curious
grounded
empowered
reflective
inspired

The publication should create the feeling:

«"I want to keep exploring."»

Guiding Principle

The publication is not merely information.

It is a visual artifact of understanding.

Every design decision should support:

meaning
atmosphere
transformation
discovery

before decoration.
