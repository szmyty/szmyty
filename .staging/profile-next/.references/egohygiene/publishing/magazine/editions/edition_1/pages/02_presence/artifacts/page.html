INT. PRINTED MAGAZINE – PRESENCE PAGE – TIMELESS
A new page.

The header arches across aged parchment:

PRESENCE

Above it,
a subtle infinity symbol glows softly,
like a quiet reminder of continuity.

Below:

Practices for Awareness & Recalibration

Centered on the page,
a silhouetted figure sits cross-legged at dusk.

Behind the figure,
faint concentric rings expand outward —
a visual frame of reference.

Four practice panels surround the figure.

ACKNOWLEDGE

Notice what sensations, thoughts, or moods arise.

BREATHE

Ground attention in the rise and fall of breath.

SPACE

Allow gaps between thoughts to gently expand.

RETURN

Frustration is normal — return to the breath.

Near the bottom,
a highlighted band reads:

EFFECT:

Calms the nervous system,
reduces reactivity,
and fosters self-awareness and equanimity.

A final line rests beneath:

Noticing grounds and soothes the system.

No motion.
No spectacle.

Instruction, embedded in artifact.

Stillness,
applied.
