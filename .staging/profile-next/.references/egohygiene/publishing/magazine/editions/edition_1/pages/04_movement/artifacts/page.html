INT. PRINTED MAGAZINE – MOVEMENT PAGE – TIMELESS
A new pillar.

The header arches across aged parchment:

MOVEMENT

Below it:
Practices for Nervous System Regulation

A subtle infinity divider rests between title and instruction.

Centered on the page,
a standing silhouette glows from beneath,
three faint echoes of the body trailing behind,
suggesting motion.

Four practice panels frame the figure.

MOVE

Change posture or position.

MOBILIZE

Invite range and circulation.

STRETCH

Ease tension without forcing range.

RELEASE

Allow excess activation to discharge.

Near the bottom,
a highlighted band reads:

EFFECT:

Regulates the nervous system,
reduces stored tension,
and restores embodied alignment.

A final declaration anchors the page:

MOVE YOUR BODY, CALM YOUR MIND,

FEEL AT HOME IN YOURSELF

The page feels warmer than stillness.
More active.
More grounded.

Energy, redirected.
