# Publishing Lifecycle

This document describes how knowledge flows through the Ego Hygiene publishing pipeline — from lived experience to archived distribution.

---

## The Pipeline

```
Experience
      ↓
Knowledge Extraction    →  synapses/
      ↓
Research                →  synapses/ + article drafts
      ↓
Article                 →  sources/articles/<slug>/draft.md
      ↓
Publication             →  sources/articles/<slug>/published.md
      ↓
Distribution            →  channels/medium/, channels/pinterest/
      ↓
Archive                 →  channels/*/manifest.json
```

---

## Stages

### 1. Experience

Knowledge originates in lived experience — observations, realizations, emotional shifts.

These are not yet captured in the repository.

### 2. Knowledge Extraction → Synapse

Raw insight is captured immediately as a Synapse.

**Location:** `sources/synapses/`

Synapses are:
- Unfiltered and unfinished
- Organized loosely by theme
- A fire to capture before it fades

A dedicated Synapse Specification and Knowledge Extraction pipeline will follow in a future issue.

### 3. Research → Article Draft

Synapses are synthesized into structured research, then developed into article drafts.

**Location:** `sources/articles/<slug>/draft.md`

Each article directory contains:
- `draft.md` — work in progress
- `THESIS.md` — the thesis and conceptual spine
- `references.md` — citations and sources
- `assets/` — images and figures

### 4. Publication → Published Article

When an article is ready, it is finalized as `published.md`.

**Location:** `sources/articles/<slug>/published.md`

`published.md` is the canonical source of truth for all distribution.

### 5. Distribution → Channels

Published articles are distributed to external channels.

**Medium:**
- Synced automatically via the Medium RSS tool
- Archive stored in `channels/medium/`
- Configured via `channels/medium/config.yaml`

**Pinterest:**
- Synced automatically via the Pinterest RSS tool
- Archive stored in `channels/pinterest/`
- Configured via `channels/pinterest/config.yaml`

**Future channels:** website, newsletter, LinkedIn

### 6. Archive

Synchronized content is archived with incremental manifests:
- `channels/medium/manifest.json` — tracks all synced Medium articles
- `channels/pinterest/boards/<id>/manifest.json` — tracks all synced Pinterest pins

---

## Authored Content Is the Source of Truth

```
sources/        ← WRITE HERE
    articles/
    synapses/
    magazine/

channels/       ← NEVER WRITE HERE MANUALLY
    medium/
    pinterest/
```

Content in `channels/` is generated by synchronization tools.

Content in `sources/` is written by humans.

---

## Tools

The publishing pipeline is supported by tools in `tools/`:

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `mindlint` | Spec-driven linter that validates article structure and compliance |
| `medium-rss` | Ingests Medium RSS feed and archives articles locally |
| `pinterest-rss` | Ingests Pinterest RSS feed and archives pins locally |

Tools are reusable independently of any specific publication channel.

---

## Specifications

All authored content follows specifications in `specs/`:

| Spec | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `article-structure.spec.md` | Article structural pattern and phases |
| `medium-compliance.spec.md` | Medium publishing compliance rules |
| `research.spec.md` | Research and citation standards |
| `visuals.spec.md` | Visual asset requirements |
| `voice.spec.md` | Brand voice and tone |
