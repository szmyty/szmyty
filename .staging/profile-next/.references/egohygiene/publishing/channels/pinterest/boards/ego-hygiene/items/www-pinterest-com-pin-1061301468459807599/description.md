# A reflective infographic exploring how trauma can act as a perceptual filter that shapes the way we interpret people, situations, and uncertainty. This piece examines threat scanning, nervous system activation, cognitive distortions, and the difference between reacting from past pain versus responding to present reality. A reminder that the lens is not your identity—it is a protective adaptation that can be recognized, understood, and softened over time. #trauma-healing #nervous-system #self-awareness #emotional-regulation #mental-health
