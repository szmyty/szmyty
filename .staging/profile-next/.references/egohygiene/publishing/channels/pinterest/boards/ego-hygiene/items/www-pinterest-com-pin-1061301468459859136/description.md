# A reflective infographic exploring the constant conversation between the gut and the brain. This piece examines the gut-brain axis, vagus nerve signaling, stress, inflammation, mood, and how digestive health can influence emotional well-being, cognition, and resilience. A reminder that mental and emotional health are not only psychological experiences—they are deeply connected to the condition of the body that supports them. #gut-health #gut-brain-axis #nervous-system #mental-wellbeing #holistic-health
