# A reflective infographic exploring the difficult reality that genuine love and genuine harm can exist within the same relationship. This piece examines trauma projection, attachment erosion, emotional overwhelm, and the ways unhealed wounds can destabilize connection. A reminder that acknowledging the love does not erase the pain, and acknowledging the pain does not erase the love. Both can be true. #attachment-healing #trauma-dynamics #relational-growth #healthy-relationships #emotional-awareness
