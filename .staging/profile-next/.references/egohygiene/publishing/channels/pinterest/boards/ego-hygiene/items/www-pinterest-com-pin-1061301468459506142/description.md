# A reflective infographic exploring how unprocessed grief, trauma, and emotional pain can become expressed through the body. This piece examines somatic storage, muscle guarding, gut-brain signaling, chronic tension, and nervous system adaptation. A reminder that symptoms are not always signs of weakness or failure—they can be messages from a body carrying experiences that have not yet found a safe way to be felt and released. #somatic-healing #trauma-healing #mind-body-connection #nervous-system #grief-and-healing
