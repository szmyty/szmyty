# A reflective infographic exploring how unprocessed pain, insecurity, or shame can be projected outward and reshape the stories people tell about others. This piece examines projection, narrative distortion, gaslighting, and the confusion that can arise when someone protects their self-image at the expense of reality. A reminder that not every story told about you belongs to you, and clarity often begins by trusting your own experience again. #projection #gaslighting-recovery #self-trust #emotional-awareness #personal-growth
