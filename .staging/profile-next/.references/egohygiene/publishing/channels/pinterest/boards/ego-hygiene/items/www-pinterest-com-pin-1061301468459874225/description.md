# A reflective infographic exploring what happens when chronic nervous system dysregulation collides with high social, professional, or personal expectations. This piece examines cognitive overload, executive dysfunction, survival-mode activation, and the experience of feeling overwhelmed by demands that once felt manageable. A reminder that capacity is biological, not moral—and overload is often a sign that the system needs support, not more pressure. #nervous-system #burnout-recovery #executive-dysfunction #stress-response #emotional-awareness
