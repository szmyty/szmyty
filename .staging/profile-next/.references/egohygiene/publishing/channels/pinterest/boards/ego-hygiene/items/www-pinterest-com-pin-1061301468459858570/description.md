# A reflective infographic exploring how shame can become woven into ideas of masculinity when vulnerability is met with ridicule, contempt, or emotional invalidation. This piece examines suppression, nervous system adaptation, emotional disconnection, and the ways people learn to mistake armor for strength. A reminder that true strength is not the absence of feeling—it is the ability to remain present, connected, and human in the face of it. #healthy-masculinity #emotional-awareness #shame-healing #self-compassion #nervous-system
