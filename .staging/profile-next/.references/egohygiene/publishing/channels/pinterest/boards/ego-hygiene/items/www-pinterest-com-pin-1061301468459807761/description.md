# A reflective infographic exploring what can happen when major life structures collapse at the same time. This piece examines role loss, identity disruption, self-narrative collapse, and the gradual process of rebuilding from deeper foundations. A reminder that feeling fragmented is not always a sign that you are falling apart—it can be a sign that an old identity is dissolving so a more authentic one can emerge. #identity-reconstruction #personal-growth #self-discovery #healing-journey #inner-transformation
