# A reflective infographic exploring how unprocessed pain can move through families, relationships, and generations when it is not consciously felt, understood, and integrated. This piece examines projection, emotional metabolization, inherited survival patterns, and the possibility of creating a different legacy through awareness and healing. A reminder that you are not responsible for what was passed to you, but you can choose what gets passed forward. #generational-trauma #trauma-healing #emotional-awareness #family-patterns #healing-journey
